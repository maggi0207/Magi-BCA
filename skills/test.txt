   ```csharp
   FormType = FormType!.Value,
   TaskId = TaskId!.Value,
   PractitionerActionFormId = _currentPafId!.Value

The attachment table has no task-ID foreign key, and the controller inserts the supplied IDs directly.

   I can’t prove the historical upload failure’s exact server-side cause: the prior authenticated multipart response/body isn’t available. The former failure branch is reached when the upload response state is not Success; causes such as authorization or blob-scan failure require that live response to distinguish. The code-side task-ID inconsistency is fixed, but a signed-in PDF upload still needs runtime confirmation.
