**2. Why 10/28 → 11/11**
The code’s default is **14 calendar days**: October 28 plus 14 days is November 11. That precisely matches the reported result if application-received/processing date was October 28. I have not queried the incident CVI to verify its stored base date.

New-practitioner NPP has another gap: it hits the earlier `Practitioner.IsNew` exclusion and receives a null due date.

**3. Reusable Business-Day Logic**
The existing Recruitment branch uses:

    startDate, startDate.AddDays(30));
