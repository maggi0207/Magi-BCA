**Gate Quotes**
Before coding, the add/update actions were dispatching directly to their strategy factories. Submit dispatched MSP users to the NPP path, CPC users to CPC processing except for Recruitment tasks, and Recruitment-role users to `ProcessSubmitForRecruitmentAsync` without checking `TaskSelected`.

The new checks return `Forbid()` for non-MSP NPP add/update requests and NPP facility-list requests. Non-MSP submit checks both the posted task and the persisted PAF task. CPC may proceed only when both identify NPP and the persisted status is `SubmittedByMsp`, `ResubmittedByMsp`, or `UnderReviewByCpc`. Recruiter strategy dispatch now requires `TaskSelected == Recruitment`.
