- Legacy task-wide License PSV is assigned only to its saved selected license.
- Sanctions and NPI PSV stay visible and task-wide. Their replacement behavior is unchanged.
- The processor attaches only the selected license’s PSV. Retained entries do not cause other Cactus licenses to be created.
- Existing required-field behavior remains, including no License PSV requirement for VA = Yes and PA = Yes.
