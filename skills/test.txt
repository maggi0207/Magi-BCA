MSP Open queue. The badges stay Saved (50) and Submitted (20), and Open stays (70), after Type is changed to NPP PAF or to Add Practitioner to Facility. The grid then shows one row. The badge totals are the whole queue. They do not match the filtered rows.

Quote where the Saved and Submitted badge counts are loaded, and whether that query receives TaskType. Quote the grid query that already applies TaskType.Npp as IndexedTaskSelected.

- Type cleared: the badges return to the current whole-queue counts.
- Do not change the NPP PAF option text. Other status chips use the same Type scope.
